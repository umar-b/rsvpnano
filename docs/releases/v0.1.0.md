# RSVP Nano v0.1.0 — umar-b build

The first big feature release of this fork. Everything below has been tested on-device.

## Highlights

- **Set-down standby**: put the device down flat and it goes to standby on its own; screen-down turns the display off entirely. Lift it to wake. A flick while reading rewinds to the previous sentence.
- **Smarter pacing**: ramp-in after every pause, long-word splitting, clause-aware pacing, and a per-book WPM memory.
- **Library quality of life**: filters (All / In progress / Unread / Finished), "Surprise me", on-device delete, per-book bookmarks.
- **Stats & streaks**: device clock, calendar-aware reading history, daily word goal, streaks, achievements, and a celebration when you finish a book.
- **Starred sentences**: star a quote while reading, browse and export it from the web companion.
- **Focus Timer**: completion jingles and reading sprints with per-block word counts; mute and volume settings.
- **New screensavers**: word rain, DVD bounce, and book cover — plus interactive Life (taps stamp gliders) and AMOLED burn-in protection.
- **Multi Wi-Fi**: up to 5 saved home networks, strongest-first connection.
- **Resilient OTA**: updates come from this fork's releases, survive marginal Wi-Fi, and the settings page shows the running version with a check-now button.
- **Purple accent** across the UI.
- Crash fix: opening a large library no longer starves the main loop (watchdog resets); the boot log now records the reset reason.
- Removed: tilt-to-scrub (didn't earn its place in real use).

## Assets

- `rsvp-nano.bin` is the full browser-flasher image for fresh installs.
- `rsvp-nano-ota.bin` is the app-only binary used by OTA updates.
